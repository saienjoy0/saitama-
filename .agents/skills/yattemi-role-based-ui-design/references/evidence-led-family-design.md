# Evidence-led family design

Use this checklist before proposing screens or AI. Read the project's active spec for exact values and scope. Treat personas as hypotheses until observed; age does not determine digital competence.

## Work backwards from three outcomes

Write the child's desired choice/discovery, the parent's reduced preparation and bounded oversight, and the grandparent's optional contribution. State each role's context, concern, first successful action, and a missing-participant fallback. Describe one before/after customer experience and answer: why use this, what can go wrong, what happens with no AI or no reply, and what evidence would reject it?

Map child question → choice/experience → record → consented sharing → family experience/question → optional seed → child-selected new experience. Preserve source and content revision across handoffs. Do not count mere delivery, messages, or longer use as learning.

## Use one design language, several compositions

Define semantic tokens for surfaces, text, actions, states, spacing, shape, type, and motion. Map them to reusable components and then role variants. Avoid scattered literal colors and durations. Child exploration, parent review, and grandparent reading should differ in hierarchy and density while sharing save/share/revoke meanings.

List component states and feedback IDs. Check normal/loading/empty/error/revoked as applicable. Do not force every widget to carry all states. Include keyboard, text enlargement and motion reduction. Do not copy Android units into web CSS or mix two complete component libraries merely because both are reputable.

## Make play support agency

Give children a reason to predict, compare, choose, change their mind, and see what happened. Let them express a discovery in their own words; keep hints optional and available. Use collections to retain their discoveries, not to reward spending or compliance. Provide calm themes for children who dislike game-like presentation. Reject coerced streaks, unpredictable rewards, family reply pressure and an AI companion that implies emotional dependence.

## Require AI-specific value

Write a fixed/rule-based baseline first. Use AI where variable language or context creates a demonstrated bottleneck. Separate selection among curated IDs from generated wording and from consequential actions. Give users edit/skip/off controls and an understandable fixed fallback. Explain actual input provenance, not invented confidence or hidden reasoning. Retain human authorship and distinguish AI output from family testimony. Do not regenerate the child's answer for them.
