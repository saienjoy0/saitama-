## Most important combined finding

The project should not currently optimize for feature breadth.

The highest-priority unknowns are:

1. **Child repeat desire** — after roughly 3–5 minutes, does the child voluntarily want another attempt?
2. **Parent repeat value** — does the parent actually facilitate a second real session rather than merely praising the idea?
3. **Child comprehension of the autonomy loop** — can the child move from situation → noticing → interpretation → proposal without adults/AI supplying the answer?
4. **Payer commitment** — after experiencing value, will a parent make a real financial commitment?
5. **Real autonomy** — when the child refuses, changes, or negotiates, is the choice actually respected within safe family boundaries?

These are more load-bearing than family newspaper format, photo submission, point systems, grandparent-first onboarding, or full-app architecture.

## Current core after first-principles stripping

Real household situation
→ child notices / interprets
→ child proposes or modifies an action
→ child can refuse/negotiate/request help
→ action is agreed
→ child acts
→ recipient states what concrete value changed
→ child reflects briefly
→ next attempt uses less scaffolding where possible

Everything else should justify itself by improving this loop or its commercial repeatability.

## Recommended immediate validation sequence

1. Manual/concierge household exploration test
2. Compare AI-apprentice onboarding against simpler non-AI variants
3. Ask for a second-session parent commitment
4. Observe natural refusal/negotiation behavior
5. After experienced value, present a concrete paid-pilot offer
6. Re-score the assumption map from real evidence

## Evidence discipline

All current JTBD opportunities and several solution branches remain hypotheses because the available sources are primarily product specifications, not completed customer research. Do not convert them into validated facts until behavior/interview evidence supports them.
