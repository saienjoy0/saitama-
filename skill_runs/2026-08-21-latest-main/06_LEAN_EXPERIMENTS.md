# Lean / Pretotype Experiments — Latest Main Spec

Applied skill: `brainstorm-experiments-new`

The thresholds below are **provisional decision thresholds for a first experiment**, not evidence-backed population estimates. They should be adjusted before fielding based on sample size and founder risk tolerance.

## XYZ hypotheses

### H1 — repeat
At least **60% of child-parent pairs who complete session 1** will voluntarily schedule/complete a second real session within 7 days without researcher pressure.

### H2 — support fading
At least **50% of pairs who complete 3 sessions** will require lower maximum combined AI + parent support in session 3 than session 1 on at least two core steps (e.g. noticing/framing/solution), without the parent substituting equivalent direct answers.

### H3 — transfer
At least **30% of pairs who complete 3–5 sessions** will bring or frame at least one new household situation initiated by the child without a concrete observation prompt from AI/parent.

### H4 — parent commitment
After experiencing the pilot, at least **20% of participating parents** will accept a specific paid continuation/deposit/test purchase when given a real opportunity to say no.

These thresholds are intended to force decisions, not to claim market prevalence.

## Experiment 1 — Longitudinal Concierge Mechanism Test

**Tests:** H1, H2, H3.

**Method**
- Recruit a small set of target child-parent pairs.
- Same pair uses 3–5 sessions with real household situations.
- Human facilitator can operate behind the interface to control the hint ladder.
- Log each cognitive step: independent / question reframe / observation lens / options / example / parent supplied answer.
- Record parent interventions separately, then calculate a combined support profile.
- Introduce at least one meaningfully different household situation to avoid mistaking task memorization for transfer.

**Metrics**
- session-2 opt-in/completion;
- maximum support intensity by step and session;
- number/type of parent interventions;
- child-initiated new situations;
- concrete value response from recipient;
- parent active time.

**Success threshold**
Use H1–H3 provisional thresholds together. A good H1 with failed H2/H3 is not core success.

## Experiment 2 — AI Apprentice vs Minimal Non-AI Scaffold

**Tests:** A8 / whether AI-apprentice is actually needed.

**Method**
Compare two lightweight onboarding variants:
- A: AI apprentice asks child to explain/correct the household situation.
- B: simple observation card/button gives one observation lens and then gets out of the way.

Do not compare visual polish. Keep the household situation and facilitator behavior as similar as possible.

**Metrics**
- time to first child-authored observation;
- highest support needed;
- child-authored proposal rate;
- voluntary desire to repeat;
- child perception of “my idea” vs “it told me what to do” (supporting qualitative signal).

**Decision**
If the simpler variant performs similarly or better on authorship and support intensity, do not make AI-apprentice a product identity.

## Experiment 3 — Parent Problem / Commitment Pretotype

**Tests:** A4, A5, H4.

**Method**
1. Before showing the product, conduct Mom Test interviews about the last time the parent wanted the child to act independently but stepped in or gave the answer.
2. Invite qualified parents to an actual 3–5 session pilot, requiring time commitment.
3. After they have experienced the service, present one concrete finite paid continuation offer.

**Metrics**
- proportion with recent, specific episodes;
- what they already tried/spend on;
- pilot acceptance and completion;
- parent active minutes/interventions;
- paid continuation/deposit acceptance;
- referral introduction.

**Skin in the game**
Time, repeat participation, introduction, and money matter more than “I love this.”

## Experiment 4 — Graduation-model probe

**Tests:** A16 business-model tension.

**Method**
After a parent has seen support fading, compare two concrete offers:
- an indefinite “keep using the app” framing;
- a finite “build independence and graduate to less support” cycle.

**Metric**
Actual choice / payment / deposit, plus why they chose it.

Do not run this before participants have experienced the underlying value; hypothetical preference is weak evidence.
